Our story

EXAMPLE

PeraPlano started in 2025 in a Quezon City apartment, with a drawer full of receipts and three budgeting apps abandoned in a row. Every one of them made the same demand: type in everything you spend, every day, forever.

But the phone already knew. Every GCash payment pinged. Every BPI debit alerted. The data was sitting right there in the notification shade — nobody was listening to it.

So we built the listener. PeraPlano turns the alerts you already get into a complete, automatic picture of your money — parsed on your phone, never uploaded, never sold. You set the rules once; it does the logging forever.

OUR MISSION

Financial clarity for every Filipino — automatic, private, and free to start.
